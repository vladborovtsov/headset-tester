ABOUT THE SWITCH

What your browser can do

Browsers cannot select a Bluetooth profile directly. Starting microphone capture normally asks the operating system to switch a compatible headset to HFP/HSP; stopping it lets high-quality A2DP playback resume.

For best results, select your Bluetooth headset for both input and output in your system settings. Use headphones to prevent feedback.
